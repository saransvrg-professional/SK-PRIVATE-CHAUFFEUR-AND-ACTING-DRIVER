## 💬 WhatsApp Status & Broadcast Marketing (Free Leads)
Your phone contacts are your warmest audience. Build an organic pipeline using status updates.

### The "Trip Story" Format (Post every 2-3 days):
*   Whenever you go on a trip (e.g., Chennai drop, Yercaud climb), post a 3-part status:
    1.  *Status 1 (Morning):* Photo of the car ready and clean. Caption: *"Ready for a trip to Chennai. Safe driving always. 🛣️"*
    2.  *Status 2 (Mid-day):* View of the highway or hills from the co-driver's/back seat (make sure it looks smooth, not shaky). Caption: *"Smooth cruising on NH. Client relaxing in the back. 🎧"*
    3.  *Status 3 (Evening):* Photo of the car safely parked at the destination. Caption: *"Destination reached safely. Happy clients! Client reviews make our day. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐"*

### Broadcast Message (Send once a month, target holiday weekends):
*   **Template:**
    > "Hi Sir/Mam, planning a trip for this weekend/holiday? Don't stress about driving through the traffic or hills. Book SK Private Chauffeur and enjoy the ride with your family. 24/7 safe and professional service. Contact: +91 96006 13619. You Relax, We Drive!"
